Mapped onto spheres; imagery has enhanced color and some reconstructed terrain. Neptune map is not a true-color rendering.

Three.js r170: MIT license, included in src/vendor/LICENSE.txt.

Educational model: illustrative sizes and circular distances, not a live ephemeris. Orbital period ratios preserved with one Earth year per 20 minutes; initial phases illustrative. Moon orbit and axial orientation simplified.
Planet descriptions and facts: NASA Science destination pages, linked individually in the viewer.

Detail upgrade: 8K maps for Mercury, Earth, Moon and Mars; 4K maps for the Sun, Jupiter, Saturn and Venus atmosphere (verified image dimensions). Uranus and Neptune retain the available 2K maps. Earth has an independent 2K cloud layer. Solar convection, corona and cloud motion are illustrative shaders, not live observations or temperature measurements. Rocky-body relief is subtle luminance-derived shading, not measured elevation.

Solar bursts use random visible-hemisphere locations and animated plasma loops, with illustrative timing and scale; they are not live solar activity.
