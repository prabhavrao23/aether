Welcome to the Milky Way

Our galaxy is a huge family of stars, gas, and dust. Its spiral arms curl around a bright central bar.

Drag to orbit, scroll to fly closer, and hold Shift while dragging to slide sideways.

This is an artistic 3D model. Each light represents a star; sizes, spacing, and motion are exaggerated so we can explore them.

Constellations are patterns we see from Earth. These patterns are embedded in this model as a learning aid. Their placements and depths are illustrative, not measured locations in the Milky Way.
